Case Study

SL Construction Program

Nye Veier + Hive Autonomy

Europe's first industrial test of autonomous machines in tunnel and quarry operations

HOW THEY MET

Through a Startuplab Construction excursion to Nye Veier in Kristiansand, where Hive Autonomy presented their autonomous machine technology.

THE PILOT

Nye Veier and Hive Autonomy ran the first test of autonomous machines in Nordic construction. A robot dog and an autonomous wheel loader operated side by side, controlled and monitored in real time from Oslo, 100 km from site.

WHY IT MATTERS

Proved that autonomous technology works in demanding construction environments with high demands for precision and safe production. Not replacing people, but making the work safer and better.

Global Trends 2024–2026

Where the world is heading

MaaS & Integration

Single-app multimodal journeys going mainstream. Public operators becoming platforms.

Key shift: From owning vehicles to orchestrating trips

Autonomous Mobility

Waymo at 150K+ rides/week. L4 on fixed corridors now viable. Europe: shuttle pilots scaling.

Key shift: From pilot to scaled deployment on defined routes

Micro-Mobility

Tier, Voi profitable after consolidation. Integration with public transit the winning model.

Key shift: Standalone → integrated first/last mile

AI in Route Planning

Real-time demand prediction, dynamic fleet allocation. 15–30% efficiency gains documented.

Investment Trends

$38B+ into mobility tech (2024). Shift from hardware to software/data layer.

Mobilitet & Autonomi

Fire selskaper som former Ruters fremtid

Fra selvkjorende busser til AI-optimert ruteplanlegging. Alle er aktive i Norden eller allerede i Ruters verdikjede.

Holo Norge

Ruters AV-operator i Groruddalen. 5 NIO ES8, 100 000+ km, ~7 000 passasjerer. Valgt MOIA/Mobileye for neste fase. 6 VW ID.Buzz AD fra sommeren 2026.

Allerede Ruters kjernepartner for selvkjoring

MOIA VW Group / Hamburg

VWs selskap for autonom ride-pooling. Leverer VW ID.Buzz AD-plattformen til Ruter via Holo. 400+ ansatte, opererer i Hamburg siden 2019. Europas storste autonome flateprosjekt.

Ruters valgte kjoretoyleverandor for 500-buss-utvidelsen

Vay Berlin / Hamburg

Teledriving = en operater fjerntyrer bilen fra et kontrollsenter via kameraer og sensorer. Passasjeren sitter i en forerfri bil, men et menneske har kontroll. Lavere kostnad enn full autonomi, raskere godkjenning.

Bro-teknologi: Supervised autonomi for omrader med lavt volum

Optibus Israel / London

AI-plattform for ruteplanlegging og flaatestyring i kollektivtransport. Brukes av 1 000+ byer globalt. Optimerer ruter, vaktplaner og elektrifisering. Raised $260M+.

AI-optimering av Ruters rutenett og overgang til el-busser
