Corporate Innovation Network

Case Study

SL Construction Program

Nye Veier + Hive Autonomy

Europe's first industrial test of autonomous machines in tunnel and quarry operations

HOW THEY MET

Through a Startuplab Construction excursion to Nye Veier in Kristiansand, where Hive Autonomy presented their autonomous machine technology.

THE PILOT

Nye Veier and Hive Autonomy ran the first test of autonomous machines in Nordic construction. A robot dog and an autonomous wheel loader operated side by side, controlled and monitored in real time from Oslo, 100 km from site.

WHY IT MATTERS

Proved that autonomous technology works in demanding construction environments with high demands for precision and safe production. Not replacing people, but making the work safer and better.
