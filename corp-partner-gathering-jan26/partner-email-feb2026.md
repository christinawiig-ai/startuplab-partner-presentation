Looking ahead to 2026

The pace of change is accelerating and with it, the risk of falling behind. Business models that worked five years ago are being disrupted, global uncertainty is reshaping markets, and the companies that thrive will be those that adapt fastest. Our role is to curate access to the most ambitious early-stage startups, concrete collaboration opportunities and competence building that help you move faster than what most organizations can build internally.

AI from awareness to organizational transformation

We now offer a structured AI rollout program that takes your employees from non-technical to building functional applications in days, not months. The program is hands-on: employees build real tools, dashboards, and automations using AI-assisted coding. Top performers advance to champion-level training and become internal teachers, making your organization self-sustaining. As a corporate partner, you get access to an initial inspiration event and foundation course at no cost.

We also host AI Mad Lab one of the fastest-growing AI developer communities in the Nordics, with monthly events drawing 200+ practitioners. It's a space where your teams can connect with cutting-edge builders and stay on top of what's actually happening in AI.

And our Hardware and Energy programs continue to deepen the connection between startups and corporate partners. International ambition & network

2025 at a glance

The startup ecosystem in context

The Norwegian startup ecosystem received significant international attention in 2025, driven by milestones such as 1X's global breakthrough in robotics, the sale of Tise to eBay a company that received early support and access to networks through Startuplab long before its acquisition Nofence raising Europe's largest investment round in AgTech, and Consigli's acquisition by Aecom.

Globally, venture capital activity strengthened throughout the year, with AI-driven companies attracting a growing share of funding. Corporate venture capital continues to play an increasingly important role, underlining the strategic value of early access to new technology.

Startuplab
- More than 800 applications to our incubator and accelerator, with a consistent admission rate under 10 percent, reflecting a highly selective pipeline of early-stage companies.
- Over 90 curated events and workshops across our offices, designed for depth, relevance, and meaningful dialogue.

Startuplab Ventures
- Closed our largest fund to date, larger than the previous four funds combined.
- Strong deal flow with several serial founders.
- Welcomed a number of new investors to the fund, including DNV, Telenor and OBOS as limited partners.

SL Energy

The Energy Program has been highly active throughout the year, combining advisory boards, Corporate Innovation Networks, and hands-on collaboration between startups and partners.

One clear example of the impact this model can deliver is the collaboration between Equinor and Fieldnode. Through close cooperation, Fieldnode's 3D-printing technology has contributed to more than 1 BNOK in documented savings for Equinor. This illustrates the type of outcomes we actively design for: concrete challenges, the right startup, and a setup that enables collaboration to move beyond pilots and into real operational impact.

The Startuplab Summit continued to grow in 2025 and has become a flagship event during Oslo Innovation Week. This year's Summit also marked the start of our Nordic expansion, through partnerships with our sister organisations in Sweden, Finland, and Denmark. The Summit brought together more than 260 Nordic startups, over 400 international investors, and more than 250 executives from established companies. A special shout-out to our corporate partners engaging directly with our startups we know several of you now have promising ongoing dialogues because of it.

SL Construction

In the Construction Program, activity has been particularly strong around solutions related to planning, execution, and zero-emission construction. Highlights included Zero Emission Construction, site visits to electrified construction projects, and Corporate Innovation Network sessions hosted by partners such as Nye Veier.

Startups such as Hive Autonomy illustrate the growing momentum around autonomous and digitalized construction sites. Through site visits, advisory boards, and direct dialogue with partners, several collaborations are now progressing from exploration to concrete next steps.

Hardware

Our Hardware Program has focused on helping founders scale advanced hardware solutions while connecting them with Norwegian manufacturing partners. We believe advanced hardware is best developed locally in the early phases, where close collaboration between founders and manufacturers is critical.

In 2025, we established Norway's first hardware advisory forum, consisting of serial entrepreneurs and manufacturers providing direct guidance to early-stage founders, complemented by field trips, meetups, and focused working sessions.

Access Matters

Last year reinforced something we believe strongly: in a market with increasing technological uncertainty and more available alternatives, access matters. Access to the right founders, the right technologies, and the right decision-makers at the right time. Happy to have you onboard!
